## 5. TYPOGRAPHY

### Primary Typeface: **Inter** (Sans-serif)
- **Usage**: UI, body text, buttons, forms
- **Weights**: Regular (400), Medium (500), Semibold (600), Bold (700)
- **Why**: Modern, highly legible on mobile, excellent for interfaces
- **License**: Open Font License (free)

### Display Typeface: **Poppins** (Sans-serif)
- **Usage**: Logo, headings, hero text, marketing materials
- **Weights**: Semibold (600), Bold (700), Extrabold (800)
- **Why**: Friendly, geometric, strong presence
- **License**: Open Font License (free)

### Monospace: **JetBrains Mono**
- **Usage**: Bet IDs, transaction references, odds display
- **Why**: Clear number differentiation, professional

### Font Hierarchy

```
H1 (Hero): Poppins Bold, 48px/56px, #1A1F2E
H2 (Section): Poppins Semibold, 36px/44px, #1A1F2E
H3 (Subsection): Poppins Semibold, 24px/32px, #1A1F2E
H4 (Card Title): Inter Semibold, 18px/28px, #1A1F2E
Body Large: Inter Regular, 16px/24px, #1A1F2E
Body: Inter Regular, 14px/20px, #1A1F2E
Small: Inter Regular, 12px/16px, #6B7280
Button: Inter Semibold, 14px/20px, uppercase
```

## 6. LOGO SPECIFICATIONS

### Logo Variations Required

**1. Primary Logo** (Full Color)
- BetEA wordmark + icon
- Use on white/light backgrounds
- Formats: SVG, PNG (transparent), PNG (white bg)

**2. Logo Mark** (Icon Only)
- Standalone icon/symbol
- Use for: app icon, favicon, social media profile
- Formats: SVG, PNG (512x512, 256x256, 128x128, 64x64, 32x32)

**3. Horizontal Logo**
- Icon + wordmark side-by-side
- Use for: website header, email signatures
- Formats: SVG, PNG

**4. Stacked Logo**
- Icon above wordmark
- Use for: mobile app splash screen, square spaces
- Formats: SVG, PNG

**5. Monochrome Versions**
- All white (for dark backgrounds)
- All black (for light backgrounds)
- Single color (green only)

**6. Inverted Logo**
- White logo on green background
- Use for: primary buttons, hero sections

### Logo Clear Space
- Minimum clear space: Height of "E" in BetEA on all sides
- Never place logo on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast

### Minimum Sizes
- Digital: 120px width (horizontal), 80px width (icon only)
- Print: 25mm width (horizontal), 15mm width (icon only)

### Logo Don'ts
- ❌ Don't stretch or distort
- ❌ Don't rotate
- ❌ Don't change colors (except approved variations)
- ❌ Don't add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines)
- ❌ Don't place on low-contrast backgrounds
- ❌ Don't recreate or modify the logo

- **Style**: Outline icons (2px stroke)

- **Size**: 20px (small), 24px (default), 32px (large)

- **Sports Photos**: High-energy action shots, diverse athletes

## 9. TONE OF VOICE

### Writing Guidelines

**Do**:
- ✅ Use active voice: "Place your bet" not "Bets can be placed"
- ✅ Be direct: "Deposit now" not "You might want to consider depositing"
- ✅ Show excitement: "You won KES 5,000!" not "Your bet was successful"
- ✅ Be transparent: "20% tax will be deducted" not hidden in fine print
- ✅ Use local language: "M-Pesa", "Paybill", "KES"
- ✅ Include Swahili where appropriate: "Karibu" (Welcome), "Asante" (Thank you)

**Don't**:
- ❌ Overpromise: "Guaranteed wins"
- ❌ Pressure: "Bet now or miss out forever"
- ❌ Jargon: "Utilize our platform" → "Use BetEA"
- ❌ Ignore risks: Always mention responsible gambling

### Example Copy

**Registration CTA**: "Join 10,000+ Winners. Sign Up in 30 Seconds."

**Deposit Success**: "KES 500 added to your wallet. Ready to win big?"

**Bet Confirmation**: "Bet placed! Good luck. We'll notify you when it settles."

**Withdrawal**: "Your KES 2,000 is on the way to your M-Pesa. Expect it in 5 minutes."

**Responsible Gambling**: "Remember: Betting should be fun. Set your limits and play responsibly."
